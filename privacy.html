Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Rulift ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Rulift mobile game (the "Game").

Information We Collect

The Game may use third-party advertising and analytics services. These services may collect device identifiers, IP address, device and operating-system information, language and region settings, app interaction data, and approximate location derived from an IP address. This information is used to measure app performance, prevent fraud, provide and measure advertising, and improve the Game.

Advertising and Tracking

The Game may request permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework. If you allow tracking, advertising partners may use the device advertising identifier to deliver and measure more relevant ads. You can change this choice at any time in your iOS Settings. Declining permission will not prevent you from playing the Game.

Third-Party Services

The Game may use Yandex Mobile Ads and AppMetrica services for advertising, measurement, analytics, and crash diagnostics. These providers process information according to their own privacy policies. We do not sell personal information for money.

How We Use Information

We use the information described above to operate and protect the Game, understand gameplay and technical performance, fix issues, measure advertising, and support the Game’s continued development.

Data Retention and Security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children’s Privacy

The Game is not directed to children under the age at which they can provide consent under applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us using the support contact listed on the Game’s App Store product page.

Your Choices

You may limit ad tracking in iOS Settings, reset your advertising identifier where available, and manage device permissions through your device settings. You may also contact us through the support contact listed on the Game’s App Store product page with privacy questions or requests.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised version on this page and update the “Last updated” date.
